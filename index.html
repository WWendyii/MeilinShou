User Behavior Data-Driven Interface Optimization Design Research for Computer Science Learning Platforms

This research presents a data-driven methodology to enhance computer science education platform interfaces through behavioral analysis. By analyzing interactive behaviors of 2,847 students across three popular platforms, we identified key behavioral indicators of learning success and developed an optimization framework that resulted in a 34% increase in task completion and a 27% improvement in knowledge retention.

Keywords: user behavior analytics; interface optimization; computer science education; e-learning platforms; adaptive UI
